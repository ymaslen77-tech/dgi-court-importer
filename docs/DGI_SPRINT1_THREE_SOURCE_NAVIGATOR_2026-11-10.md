## Non-negotiable publication rule

Before any *new or edited substantive* legal claim, procedure, case-outcome
analysis, official news fact or generated legal explanation is released, collect
and independently compare **three** exact official publications from three
**different publishing authorities**. Record the verbatim passages, precise
URLs, check times, meaning, applicability, time period and discrepancy check
per statement. If the sources disagree, point to different provisions or do not
all support the same proposition, the claim stays outside public release.
More links to one institution are not additional corroboration. No invented
citations, effective dates, legal arguments or approval records.

This is a source policy, not an assertion that every existing legacy article
has already passed it. The 33 owner-authorized pages, 26 existing news and 44
static source court records are unchanged by the sprint; the re-audit backlog
is separate from existing publication permission.
